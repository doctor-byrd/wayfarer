# STARTER ADVENTURE: The Saffron Run

## Adventure Overview

**Premise:** The party is hired to escort a valuable cargo of saffron from the frontier mining town of Copperhollow to the distant metropolis of Valenmere in time for the Harvest Festival. The saffron is worth a fortune, but the road is long, the weather is turning, and a rival merchant caravan is racing to deliver their own cargo first.

**Themes:** Trade, logistics, rivalry, the rhythm of the road, weather as antagonist.

**Play Time:** 2–3 sessions (8–12 hours).

**Adventure Level:** 1 (suitable for starting characters).

**Key Systems Demonstrated:**
- Full Journey/Adventure cycle (5 days of travel)
- Trade & Economy (buying at frontier, selling at metropolis)
- Weather & terrain mechanics
- Logistics (pack animals, burden, supplies)
- Parley & Posture (multiple factions: bandits, toll collectors, rival merchants)
- Heart system (Desire/Fear triggers tied to the journey)
- Track 1 Character-Built Adversary (the rival merchant)
- Track 2 Framework Adversaries (bandits, wild beasts, weather hazards)

---

## Part 1: The Hook

### Copperhollow

The party begins in **Copperhollow**, a small frontier mining town nestled in the hills. The air smells of smoke and ore. Miners trudge through muddy streets, and the local inn, **The Pick & Shovel**, is the social hub.

**Settlement Tag:** **Frontier** (Crafted goods sell well, Rare goods are very expensive).

### The Job

**Mistress Elara Voss**, a shrewd merchant from Valenmere, is in town to buy saffron from the local growers. She needs it delivered to Valenmere before the Harvest Festival in 5 days, but she cannot make the journey herself.

> *"I've purchased 10 Units of saffron—worth 200 Crowns at current market prices. I need it in Valenmere by sundown on the fifth day. The Harvest Festival buyers will pay triple if it arrives fresh and on time. I'll pay you 50 Crowns for the delivery, plus expenses. But be warned: the road is dangerous this time of year, and there's another caravan racing to deliver their own cargo. If they arrive first, the buyers will have already spent their coin."*

**Elara's Heart:**
- **Desire:** "To secure a monopoly on saffron trade in Valenmere."
- **Fear:** "Being undercut by her rival, Silas Greymark."

### The Cargo & Logistics

The saffron is packed in 10 small crates. Each crate is 1 Unit of Rare/Luxury goods (Base Price 20 Crowns, Burden 1). Total Burden: 10.

**Logistics:**
- The party needs a **Pack Animal** (+20 Burden capacity) to carry the cargo. Elara provides a mule named **Bess** (costs 5 Crowns/week to feed, but Elara covers this).
- The party must also carry their own supplies (food, water, camping gear). If they don't have enough, they must buy more in Copperhollow (Provisions are expensive here: +50% price).
- The party can also buy additional goods in Copperhollow to sell in Valenmere (see Trade Opportunity below).

### The Reward

- **50 Crowns** for successful delivery.
- **10% of the profit** if the saffron sells for more than 200 Crowns (incentivizing the party to get a good price).
- **Reputation:** Elara will recommend the party to other merchants in Valenmere (+1 step on Commerce Checks in Valenmere for the next 3 Adventures).

### Why the Party Cares

Ask each player: **Why does your character take this job?**
- The Courier loves the open road and the challenge of a tight deadline.
- The Envoy sees this as a chance to build connections with a wealthy merchant.
- The Tinker wants to study the saffron trade and maybe invest in it.
- The Beast-keeper is excited to work with Bess the mule.

If a player's **Desire** aligns with the job (e.g., "To prove I can handle high-stakes deliveries"), award them 1 Hope Token.

## Part 2: The Journey (5 Days)

The route from Copperhollow to Valenmere is 5 days of travel through varied terrain. The party must make daily checks and manage their resources.

### The Route

| Day | Terrain | March TN | Wayfinding TN | Encampment TN | Weather |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Open Country | 6 | 6 | 7 | Clear |
| 2 | Wild Downs | 7 | 7 | 8 | Rain/Wind (−1 step) |
| 3 | Hard Lands | 8 | 8 | 9 | Overcast |
| 4 | Open Country | 6 | 6 | 7 | Clear |
| 5 | Open Country | 6 | 6 | 7 | Clear |

### Day 1: Open Country (Smooth Start)

The first day is uneventful. The roads are well-maintained, and the weather is Clear (+0).

**Encounter: The Toll Collector**

At a bridge crossing, a gruff toll collector demands 2 Crowns per person to cross. The party can:
- **Pay the toll** (2 Crowns per person).
- **Parley** (TN 7 Wit + Haggling or Resolve + Intimidation): Reduce the toll to 1 Crown per person, or convince the collector to let them cross for free.
- **Find a ford** (TN 8 Wit + Wayfinding): Cross the river elsewhere, avoiding the toll but adding 1 day to the journey.

**Trade Opportunity:**
Before leaving Copperhollow, the party can buy goods to sell in Valenmere:
- **Ore Ingots** (Crafted, Base Price 15 Crowns, Burden 2): Frontier tag means they can buy at −25% (11 Crowns each). In Valenmere (Metropolis), they can sell at +50% (22 Crowns each). Profit: 11 Crowns per Unit.
- **Miner's Tools** (Crafted, Base Price 10 Crowns, Burden 2): Buy at 8 Crowns, sell at 15 Crowns. Profit: 7 Crowns per Unit.

Encourage the party to use their Burden capacity wisely. If they have a pack animal, they can carry more.

### Day 2: Wild Downs (Weather Turns)

The terrain becomes rougher, and the weather shifts to **Rain/Wind** (all checks −1 step). The roads are muddy, and visibility is poor.

**Encounter: The Mudslide**

A section of the road has been washed out by the rain. The party must:
- **Clear the road** (TN 8 Might + Labor): Takes 1 day, but allows them to continue.
- **Find a detour** (TN 8 Wit + Wayfinding): Adds 1 day to the journey but avoids the mudslide.
- **Push through** (TN 9 Grace + Animal Handling for the mule): Risky. Failure means the mule gets stuck, and the party must spend 1 day freeing it (losing a day of travel).

**Heart Trigger:**
If a character struggles with the weather or the mudslide, the GM can offer a **Fear trigger**: "Do you accept a new Fear: 'Fear of being trapped' or 'Fear of the wilderness'? If so, gain 1 Aura or 1 Hope Token."

### Day 3: Hard Lands (The Rival Appears)

The terrain is rough, and the party encounters another caravan on the road. It's **Silas Greymark**, Elara's rival, leading his own saffron delivery.

**Silas Greymark** *(Track 1 Character-Built Adversary, Adventure Level 1)*
- **Facets:** Might d6, Grace d6, Wit d10, Resolve d8
- **Discipline:** Savant | **Job:** Envoy (Antagonist version)
- **Vitality:** 20 | **Wounds:** 0 | **Aura:** 4/4
- **Heart:**
  - **Desire:** "To deliver his saffron first and secure the Valenmere market."
  - **Fear:** "Being seen as a failure by his employers."
- **Talents:** *Silver Tongue* (reroll a failed social check), *Cultural Chameleon* (treat one hostile faction as neutral).

**The Confrontation:**

Silas approaches the party with a smirk. He has 2 guards (Track 2 Standard) and a wagon full of saffron.

**Parley Opportunity:**
Silas is arrogant and competitive. The party can:
- **Appeal to his Desire:** "Why race us? We can work together and split the profit." (TN 9, as he's focused on winning).
- **Exploit his Fear:** "Your employers will be furious if you're late. Maybe you should take the safe route." (TN 7, as he fears failure).
- **Challenge him:** "Let's see who's faster. Loser buys the winner a drink in Valenmere." (TN 7 Wit + Haggling).

**Parley Outcomes:**
- **High Crit (Boon):** Silas is intrigued by the partnership idea. He agrees to share information about the road ahead (e.g., a bridge is out on the main route).
- **Success:** Silas agrees to a friendly wager. The race is on, but no hard feelings.
- **Failure:** Silas sneers. "May the best caravan win." He rides ahead, gaining a 1-day head start.
- **Fumble (Bane):** Silas signals his guards to "accidentally" block the road. The party must fight or find a detour (losing 1 day).

**If Combat Occurs:**
The 2 guards attack. The party can use the environment (muddy road, nearby trees) to their advantage. If the party wins, Silas flees, vowing revenge.

### Day 4: Open Country (The Shortcut)

The weather clears, and the party is back on schedule. However, they discover that the main road is blocked by a fallen tree (or the bridge is out, as Silas warned).

**The Choice:**
- **Take the main road** (clear the obstacle, TN 8 Might + Labor, takes 1 day).
- **Take the shortcut** (TN 8 Wit + Wayfinding): A narrow, dangerous path through the hills. Saves 1 day, but the mule must succeed on a TN 7 Grace + Animal Handling check or risk injury (if failed, Bess takes 1 Wound and the party must slow down).

**Heart Trigger:**
If the party takes the shortcut and Bess is injured, the Beast-keeper can trigger a **Fear**: "Fear of losing their animal companion." If accepted, gain 1 Aura or 1 Hope Token.

### Day 5: Open Country (The Final Stretch)

The last day of travel. Valenmere is in sight. The party must make one final **March Check** (TN 6) to arrive on time.

**Encounter: The Bandit Ambush**

Just outside Valenmere, a group of bandits (3× Track 2 Standard) ambush the party, demanding the saffron.

**Bandits** *(Track 2 Standard)*
- **Vitality:** 22 | **Evasion:** 11 | **Armor:** 1
- **Strike:** d6 + 2 (Rusty swords)
- **Desire:** "Your saffron and coin."
- **Fear:** "The city guard or a well-armed caravan."
- **Traits:** *Ambusher* (free Action on surprise); *Surrender* (may Parley when at half Vitality).

**Tactical Notes:**
- The bandits start in the **Far** zone and charge to **Engaged**.
- The party can use the environment (roadside ditch, nearby trees) for cover or environmental interaction.
- If the party wins, they can interrogate the bandits (TN 7 Wit + Intimidation) to learn that Silas hired them to delay the party.

## Part 3: Arrival in Valenmere

### The City

Valenmere is a sprawling metropolis, bustling with festival preparations. The streets are decorated with banners, and merchants are setting up stalls.

**Settlement Tag:** **Metropolis** (Crafted/Rare goods sell well, Provisions are expensive).

### The Trade

The party arrives at the market square, where buyers are gathering for the Harvest Festival. The saffron is in high demand.

**Commerce Check:**
The party makes a **Paired Roll** (Wit/Resolve + Haggling/Appraisal, TN 7) to sell the saffron.

**Outcomes:**
- **High Crit (Boon):** A wealthy buyer offers 30 Crowns per Unit (300 Crowns total). The party earns 50 Crowns (delivery fee) + 100 Crowns (30% of profit) = 150 Crowns.
- **Success:** The saffron sells for 25 Crowns per Unit (250 Crowns total). The party earns 50 Crowns + 50 Crowns (10% of profit) = 100 Crowns.
- **Failure:** The saffron sells for the base price (20 Crowns per Unit, 200 Crowns total). The party earns only the 50 Crown delivery fee.
- **Fumble (Bane):** A buyer claims the saffron is tainted and refuses to pay. The party must find another buyer (takes 1 day) or sell it at a loss (15 Crowns per Unit).

**Additional Goods:**
If the party bought Ore Ingots or Miner's Tools in Copperhollow, they can sell them now:
- **Ore Ingots:** Sell for 22 Crowns each (profit: 11 Crowns per Unit).
- **Miner's Tools:** Sell for 15 Crowns each (profit: 7 Crowns per Unit).

### The Rival's Fate

If Silas arrived first, he has already sold his saffron and is celebrating. He smirks at the party but honors the wager (buys them a drink).

If the party arrived first, Silas is furious. He vows to sabotage them in the future (a recurring villain).

---

## Part 4: Resolution

### If the Delivery is Successful

Elara is overjoyed. She pays the party their fee and offers them a standing contract for future deliveries.

**Rewards:**
- **50–150 Crowns** (depending on Commerce Check).
- **Reputation:** +1 step on Commerce Checks in Valenmere for the next 3 Adventures.
- **Heart Trigger:** If a character's **Desire** was to prove themselves or secure a profitable deal, they fulfill it and can archive it, gaining +1 Max Aura, +2 Max Vitality, or 1 Milestone.

### If the Delivery is Late

Elara is disappointed but pays half the fee (25 Crowns). The party's reputation suffers (−1 step on Commerce Checks in Valenmere for the next Adventure).

### If the Saffron is Lost

Elara is furious and demands compensation. The party must either pay her back (200 Crowns) or face legal trouble (a future Adventure hook).

### Epilogue

As the party enjoys the Harvest Festival, ask each player:
- **What did you learn about the road?**
- **Did you make any friends or enemies?**
- **What will you do with your earnings?**

This is a perfect moment to trigger the **Heart system**: if a character gained a new Fear during the journey (e.g., "Fear of being trapped"), they can choose to confront it in a future Adventure.

## GM Notes & Tips

### Pacing
- **Day 1:** Smooth travel, introduce Trade mechanics, let players buy goods.
- **Day 2:** Weather complication, demonstrate environmental hazards.
- **Day 3:** The rival appears, Parley or combat, build tension.
- **Day 4:** The shortcut choice, logistical challenge.
- **Day 5:** The bandit ambush, final combat.
- **Part 3:** Arrival, Trade mechanics, resolution.

### Scaling
- If the party is **Adventure Level 2 or higher**, increase the bandits to **Elite** tier (Vitality 50).
- If the party is small (1–2 players), reduce the bandits to 2 instead of 3.
- If the party is large (5–6 players), add 1–2 more bandits or give Silas an additional guard.

### Hooks for Future Adventures
- **Silas's Revenge:** Silas hires bandits to sabotage the party's next delivery.
- **The Tainted Saffron:** If the party failed the Commerce Check, they must investigate why the buyer claimed it was tainted.
- **Elara's Contract:** Elara offers the party a more dangerous delivery (e.g., smuggling contraband).
- **The Bandits:** If the party let the bandits go, they return for revenge.

### Why This Adventure Works:
1. **Showcases Travel:** The full 5-day Journey cycle is demonstrated, with weather, terrain, and logistics all playing a role.
2. **Trade Mechanics:** Players buy low in Copperhollow and sell high in Valenmere, demonstrating the Settlement Tag system.
3. **Rivalry:** Silas Greymark is a Track 1 adversary with a Heart, making the competition personal and engaging.
4. **Multiple Solutions:** Players can race, negotiate, or fight their way through. Every choice matters.
5. **Tests All Mechanics:** Parley, Trade, Investigation, Heart triggers, tactical combat, logistics, weather.
6. **Seeds for the Future:** Silas can return, Elara can offer more contracts, the bandits can seek revenge.

This adventure perfectly captures the "the road is the adventure" pillar and shows that even a simple delivery can lead to a memorable, multi-session campaign.
