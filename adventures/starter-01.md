# STARTER ADVENTURE: The Missing Recipe of the Golden Goose

## Adventure Overview

**Premise:** The beloved tavern "The Golden Goose" is preparing for the annual Founder's Day festival, but disaster strikes: the tavern owner's secret recipe book—containing the legendary "Founder's Ale" recipe—has gone missing! Without it, the tavern can't serve the traditional ale that draws crowds from across the city. The party is hired to find it before the festival begins at sundown.

**Themes:** Mystery, friendly rivalry, city life, community spirit.

**Play Time:** 1–2 sessions (4–8 hours).

**Adventure Level:** 1 (suitable for starting characters).

**Key Systems Demonstrated:**
- City-based Adventure cycle (Networking, Gathering Information, Securing Lodging)
- Investigation and social encounters
- Parley & Posture (multiple negotiation opportunities)
- Heart system (Desire/Fear triggers)
- Trade & Economy (buying information, supplies)
- Tactical combat with environmental interaction (the bar fight!)
- Track 2 Framework Adversaries (drunk patrons, rival tavern staff)

---

## Part 1: The Hook

### The Golden Goose Tavern

The party starts the adventure in **The Golden Goose**, a cozy, bustling tavern in the **Market Ward** of Aizan. The tavern is decorated with warm wood, hanging lanterns, and the smell of roasting meats and fresh bread.

**Settlement Tag:** **Metropolis** (Crafted/Rare goods sell well, Provisions are expensive due to import costs).

### The Problem

**Bramble "Goose" Thornwood**, the tavern owner, is a stout, jolly man with a magnificent mustache and a perpetually flour-dusted apron. He's clearly distressed.

> *"Wayfarers! Thank the roads you're here. I'm in a right pickle. Tomorrow is Founder's Day, and my recipe book—the one with the secret Founder's Ale recipe—is gone! Stolen, I tell you! Without it, I can't brew the ale, and the festival will be a disaster. The whole city looks forward to that ale!"*

**Bramble's Heart:**
- **Desire:** "To make this Founder's Day the best one yet and honor the tavern's legacy."
- **Fear:** "That the tavern will fail and he'll lose everything his family built."

### The Reward

Bramble offers:
- **20 Crowns** for the book's return.
- **Free lodging and meals** at The Golden Goose for a month (Comfortable Lifestyle, no upkeep cost).
- **A lifetime supply of Founder's Ale** (a minor but cherished perk).

### Why the Party Cares

Ask each player: **Why does your character want to help?**
- The Chef wants to learn the secret recipe.
- The Envoy sees this as a chance to build connections with a beloved local institution.
- The Troubadour wants to be the hero of the festival.
- The Cartographer is mapping the city and needs a reason to explore different districts.

If a player's **Desire** aligns with helping Bramble, award them 1 Hope Token.

## Part 2: The Investigation

Bramble explains:
- The book was last seen yesterday evening on the bar.
- He stepped into the cellar for 10 minutes to check the barrels.
- When he returned, the book was gone.
- The tavern was busy, and many people were coming and going.
- He suspects one of three rival tavern owners might have stolen it to sabotage The Golden Goose.

### The Three Suspects

Bramble names three rival tavern owners who might benefit from The Golden Goose's failure:

1. **Mistress Vela** of **The Silver Chalice** (Noble Quarter) — Elegant, snobbish, serves expensive wines.
2. **Garrick Stonefist** of **The Iron Anvil** (Forge Ward) — Gruff, competitive, serves hearty stouts.
3. **Lysa Quickfingers** of **The Rusty Dagger** (Undercity) — Sly, cunning, serves cheap grog and hosts gambling.

### The City Districts

The party can explore four districts of Aizan, each with its own Settlement Tag and opportunities:

#### 1. Market Ward (Where They Start)
**Settlement Tag:** **Metropolis**
- Bustling streets, merchants, street performers.
- **Networking Check** (TN 6 Wit + Local Customs): Learn that a suspicious hooded figure was seen leaving The Golden Goose around the time the book vanished.
- **Trade Opportunity:** The party can buy "information" from street urchins (5 Coppers per rumor, Commerce Check TN 7 to get useful info).

#### 2. Noble Quarter
**Settlement Tag:** **Metropolis** (but with **Arcane/Academic** influences)
- Wide streets, grand estates, guarded gates.
- **The Silver Chalice:** Mistress Vela is haughty but not a thief. She denies involvement but will share gossip if the party buys her a rare wine (10 Crowns).
- **Gathering Information Check** (TN 7 Wit + Etiquette): Learn that Vela was at a noble's banquet all evening—she has an alibi.
- **Clue:** Vela mentions she saw Garrick Stonefist arguing with Bramble yesterday about "festival preparations."

#### 3. Forge Ward
**Settlement Tag:** **Frontier** (Crafted goods sell well)
- Smoky, industrial, loud hammering. Dwarves and smiths abound.
- **The Iron Anvil:** Garrick is gruff but honest. He admits he argued with Bramble, but only about who would serve the best ale at the festival.
- **Parley Opportunity:** Garrick is competitive. The party can challenge him to a drinking contest (Opposed Paired Roll, Might + Resolve) to earn his respect. If they win, he reveals he saw Lysa Quickfingers lurking near The Golden Goose yesterday.
- **Trade Opportunity:** The Forge Ward has excellent Crafted goods. The party can sell any weapons or armor they brought at +50% price.

#### 4. Undercity
**Settlement Tag:** **Lawless** (Contraband sells well, all other goods unpredictable)
- Dimly lit, narrow alleys, suspicious locals.
- **The Rusty Dagger:** Lysa is sly and evasive. She denies stealing the book but is clearly hiding something.
- **Parley Opportunity:** Lysa will talk if the party meets her **Desire**: "A cut of the festival profits." The party can negotiate (TN 9 Wit + Haggling) to offer her 5 Crowns from Bramble's reward. If they succeed, she admits she *did* take the book—but only to copy the recipe, not to keep it. She left it in a hiding spot and will reveal where if the party pays her.
- **Complication:** If the party refuses to pay, Lysa signals her thugs, and a fight breaks out (see Part 4).

## Part 3: The Twist

### The Truth

If the party investigates thoroughly, they discover the truth:

**Bramble himself misplaced the book.** In his excitement for the festival, he moved it to a "safe spot" in the cellar and forgot. The hooded figure people saw was just Bramble's apprentice, who was running an errand.

However, Lysa *did* find the book in the cellar and took it to copy the recipe. She intended to return it but got distracted.

### The Revelation

When the party confronts Lysa (or when they search the cellar of The Golden Goose with a TN 7 Wit + Notice check), they find the book hidden behind a barrel.

**Moral Dilemma:**
- **Expose Lysa:** She's technically a thief, but she didn't mean harm. Bramble will be furious and might press charges.
- **Let it slide:** Lysa is grateful and offers the party a discount at The Rusty Dagger (or a favor in the future).
- **Negotiate:** The party can broker a deal where Lysa shares her copied recipe with Bramble in exchange for a partnership (The Golden Goose and The Rusty Dagger will both serve Founder's Ale).

## Part 4: The Bar Fight

### The Escalation

Whether the party exposes Lysa or refuses to pay her, things escalate. Lysa's thugs (or a group of drunk patrons from The Rusty Dagger who followed the party) confront them in The Golden Goose.

**The Scene:**
The tavern is crowded with festival preparations. Tables are scattered, a fireplace roars, and a chandelier hangs overhead. Bramble is behind the bar, shouting for everyone to calm down.

### The Combatants

**Drunk Patrons** (2–3) *(Track 2 Nuisance)*
- **Vitality:** 12 | **Evasion:** 10 | **Armor:** 0
- **Strike:** d4 + 1 (Fists, chairs, bottles)
- **Desire:** "To cause trouble and drink for free."
- **Fear:** "The city guard arriving."
- **Traits:** *Unsteady* (−1 step to all attacks); *Brawler* (gain +1 step if they grapple an opponent).

**Lysa's Thugs** (2) *(Track 2 Standard)*
- **Vitality:** 22 | **Evasion:** 11 | **Armor:** 1 (Leather)
- **Strike:** d6 + 2 (Daggers, sap)
- **Desire:** "To protect Lysa and teach the party a lesson."
- **Fear:** "Being outmatched or losing a fight in front of their boss."
- **Traits:** *Sneaky* (gain +1 step on attacks if they surprise the party); *Quick Escape* (can flee to the Far zone without provoking a Reaction).

### Tactical Environment

The tavern is a **tactical playground**:
- **Tables:** Provide cover (+1 step to Evasion if behind a table). Can be flipped as an Environmental Interaction (TN 7 Might check) to create a barrier or knock enemies prone.
- **Fireplace:** Dangerous. A player can shove an enemy into the fireplace (TN 9 Grace check) to deal 1 Wound and force them to spend an Action escaping.
- **Chandelier:** Hanging overhead. A player can cut the rope (TN 7 Grace check with a blade) to drop it on a group of enemies (TN 9 Grace check to avoid, takes 1d4 Vitality damage and is Knocked Down).
- **Bar Bottles:** A player can grab a bottle (free Action) and use it as an improvised weapon (d4 damage) or throw it at an enemy (TN 7 Grace check, d4 damage on hit).
- **Slippery Floor:** If ale is spilled (GM's discretion or via Environmental Interaction), the area becomes Difficult Terrain (−1 step to Movement).

### Combat Flow

1. **Initiative:** The thugs/patrons start in the **Near** zone. The party is at the bar or tables.
2. **First Round:** The thugs/patrons charge to **Engaged** and attack.
3. **Environmental Interaction:** Encourage players to use the environment. "Do you want to flip that table? Grab a bottle? Shove someone into the fireplace?"
4. **Bramble's Reaction:** Bramble cowers behind the bar but will throw bottles at anyone who damages his tavern (TN 7 Grace check, d4 damage).
5. **City Guard:** If the fight lasts more than 3 rounds, 2 City Guards (Track 2 Standard) arrive and attempt to break it up. The party can Parley with them (TN 7 Wit + Etiquette) to avoid arrest, or they can flee.

### Ending the Fight

The fight ends when:
- All thugs/patrons are reduced to 0 Vitality or flee.
- The party successfully Parleys with the thugs (TN 7 Wit + Intimidation or Haggling).
- The city guard breaks it up (if the fight drags on).

## Part 5: Resolution

### If the Book is Returned

Bramble is overjoyed. He pays the party 20 Crowns and offers them free lodging and meals for a month.

**Trade Opportunity:** The party can now buy Founder's Ale at cost (1 Crown per keg) and sell it in other districts at a profit (Metropolis prices: 3 Crowns per keg).

**Reputation:** The party gains +1 step on all social checks in the Market Ward for the next 3 Adventures.

**Heart Trigger:** If a character's **Desire** was to help Bramble or prove themselves, they fulfill it and can archive it, gaining +1 Max Aura, +2 Max Vitality, or 1 Milestone.

### If Lysa is Exposed

Lysa is arrested by the city guard. The party gains a reputation as "lawful" but makes enemies in the Undercity (−1 step on social checks there).

### If Lysa is Let Go

Lysa is grateful and offers the party a favor. She reveals the location of a hidden stash of contraband in the Undercity (a future Adventure hook).

### The Festival

The Founder's Day festival begins at sundown. The tavern is packed, the ale flows freely, and the party is celebrated as heroes.

**Epilogue Questions:**
- What did the party learn about Aizan?
- Did they make any friends or enemies?
- What will they do next?

## GM Notes & Tips

### Pacing
- **Part 1:** Introduce the hook, establish the tone, let players ask questions.
- **Part 2:** Exploration and investigation. Let players choose which districts to visit. Don't force them to visit all three—let them follow the clues.
- **Part 3:** The twist. Let the players piece it together or reveal it through a successful check.
- **Part 4:** The bar fight. This is the climax—make it fun, chaotic, and tactical. Encourage environmental interaction.
- **Part 5:** Resolution. Tie up loose ends, award rewards, set up future Adventures.

### Scaling
- If the party is **Adventure Level 2 or higher**, add one more thug or increase the thugs' Vitality to 28.
- If the party is small (1–2 players), reduce the thugs to **Nuisance** tier (Vitality 12).
- If the party is large (5–6 players), add 1–2 more drunk patrons.

### Hooks for Future Adventures
- **Lysa's Favor:** If the party let Lysa go, she calls in the favor—help her pull off a heist in the Noble Quarter.
- **The Contraband Stash:** If Lysa revealed the stash, the party can investigate (and potentially claim it).
- **The City Guard:** If the guard arrested Lysa, they might recruit the party for a future mission.
- **Rivalry:** Garrick or Vela might challenge the party to a future competition.
