# STARTER ADVENTURE: The Village in Shadow

## Adventure Overview

**Premise:** The small agrarian village of Ashenford is suffering from a mysterious blight. Crops wither overnight, livestock sicken, and the villagers whisper of a curse. The party is hired to investigate and find a solution.

**Themes:** Investigation, moral ambiguity, the cost of magic, community vs. isolation.

**Play Time:** 1–2 sessions (4–8 hours).

**Adventure Level:** 1 (suitable for starting characters).

**Key Systems Demonstrated:**
- Journey/Adventure cycle (travel, weather, encampment)
- Investigation and social encounters
- Tactical combat with environmental interaction
- Parley & Posture (multiple negotiation opportunities)
- Heart system (Desire/Fear triggers)
- Trade & Economy (resupplying, settlement tags)
- Track 1 Character-Built Adversary (the Corruptor)
- Track 2 Framework Adversaries (blighted beasts)

---

## Part 1: The Hook

### The Letter

The party receives a letter (or hears a rumor in a tavern) from **Elder Maren** of Ashenford:

> *"To any Wayfarers of good heart and steady hand,*
> 
> *Our village is dying. A blight has taken our fields. The wheat turns black before it can be harvested. Our sheep cough blood. Children fall ill with fevers that no herbalist can break.*
> 
> *We are a poor village, but we have scraped together 30 Crowns for anyone who can lift this curse. We beg you: come to Ashenford. Speak with the people. Walk the blighted grove to the east. Find what has taken root in our soil.*
> 
> *May the roads bring you swift.*
> 
> *—Elder Maren"*

### Why the Party Accepts

Ask each player: **Why does your character care?** This is a perfect moment to tie into their **Heart (Desire/Fear)**:
- The Herbalist sees this as a chance to prove their skill.
- The Envoy wants to build a reputation as a problem-solver.
- The Cartographer is mapping this region and needs local guides.
- The Spellblade senses magical corruption and wants to confront it.

If a player's **Desire** aligns with helping the village, award them 1 Hope Token for leaning into their motivation.

## Part 2: The Journey to Ashenford

### The Route

Ashenford lies 3 days' travel through **Open Country** (Terrain Level 2) and then into the **Wild Downs** (Terrain Level 3) as the party approaches the village.

**Daily Cycle:**
1. **March Check** (TN 6 for Open Country, TN 7 for Wild Downs)
2. **Wayfinding Check** (TN 6, as the roads are well-marked but the final approach is through overgrown paths)
3. **Encampment Check** (TN 7, as the Wild Downs are damp and unsettled)
4. **Watch & Wonder** (GM introduces encounters)

### Day 1: Open Country (Smooth Travel)

The first day is uneventful. The roads are well-maintained, and the weather is Clear (+0). This is a good time for the party to:
- Discuss their approach to the investigation.
- Trade in a small wayside village (Agrarian tag): buy Provisions cheap, sell any Crafted goods they brought.
- Make a **Commerce Check** (Wit/Resolve + Haggling/Appraisal, TN 7) if they want to buy supplies for the village as a goodwill gesture.

### Day 2: Into the Wild Downs (Complications Begin)

The terrain becomes rougher. The roads are overgrown, and the weather shifts to **Rain/Wind** (all checks −1 step).

**Encounter: The Blighted Wolf** *(Track 2 Standard)*

As the party makes camp, a massive wolf emerges from the fog. Its fur is patchy and blackened, its eyes glow with a sickly green light. It is not natural.

> **Blighted Wolf** *(Standard)*
> - **Vitality:** 25 | **Evasion:** 11 | **Armor:** 1
> - **Strike:** d8 + 2 (Rending Claws)
> - **Desire:** "To spread the blight through pain and infection."
> - **Fear:** "Fire or the scent of healthy, living plants."
> - **Traits:** 
>   - *Blighted Bite:* On a successful hit, the target must succeed on a TN 7 Resolve check or take 1 Wound from the spreading corruption.
>   - *Pack Hunter:* If another Blighted Wolf is Engaged with the same target, this wolf gains +1 step on attacks.

**Tactical Notes:**
- The wolf emerges from the **Far** zone and charges to **Engaged** on its first turn.
- The campfire is an **Environmental Interaction** opportunity: a player can kick burning embers at the wolf (TN 7 Grace check) to drive it back 1 Zone and grant +1 step to the next attack against it.
- If the Herbalist or Spellblade is present, they can recognize the blight as magical corruption (TN 7 Wit + Lore check).

**After the Fight:**
- If the party wins, they can examine the wolf's body. A TN 7 Wit + Lore check reveals that the blight is not natural—it's the work of a **Corruptor** (someone who has twisted the Root pillar).
- If a character is injured by the **Blighted Bite**, this is a perfect moment for the GM to offer a **Heart trigger**: "Do you accept a new Fear: 'Fear of the Blight' or 'Fear of corruption'? If so, gain 1 Aura or 1 Hope Token."

### Day 3: Arrival at Ashenford

The party arrives at Ashenford, a small cluster of thatched-roof cottages surrounding a central well. The fields are blackened and wilted. The villagers are gaunt and wary.

**Settlement Tag:** **Agrarian** (Provisions are cheap to buy here, but the blight has ruined most of the stock. Crafted goods are expensive.)

**Elder Maren** greets the party. She is old, tired, but dignified.
- **Desire:** "To save her people, even at great personal cost."
- **Fear:** "That the blight will spread to neighboring villages and she will be remembered as the one who failed."

She explains:
- The blight started 2 weeks ago in the **Blighted Grove** to the east.
- A stranger arrived a month ago—a hooded figure who asked to study the old standing stones in the grove. The villagers agreed, hoping for trade.
- Three days after the stranger arrived, the blight began.
- The stranger is still in the grove. No one dares approach.

**Investigation Phase:**
The party can:
1. **Talk to Villagers** (TN 7 Wit + Local Customs or Resolve + Empathy): Learn that the stranger was seen performing strange rituals at night, pouring black liquid into the soil.
2. **Examine the Blighted Fields** (TN 7 Wit + Lore or Resolve + Nature): Recognize the blight as magical corruption tied to the Root pillar. The corruption is spreading from the grove.
3. **Trade with the Village** (Commerce Check, TN 7): The village has almost nothing to sell, but if the party brought medicine or food, they can sell it at a premium (Metropolis-level prices due to desperation).

## Part 3: The Blighted Grove

The grove is a circle of ancient standing stones, now overgrown with blackened, thorny vines. The air is thick with a sickly-sweet smell. At the center, a hooded figure tends to a pulsing, blackened root that has erupted from the earth.

### The Corruptor: Sylas the Root-Twisted

> **Sylas, the Root-Twisted** *(Track 1 Character-Built Adversary, Adventure Level 1)*
> - **Facets:** Might d6, Grace d6, Wit d8, Resolve d10
> - **Discipline:** Savant | **Job:** Corruptor (Root)
> - **Vitality:** 22 | **Wounds:** 0 | **Aura:** 5/5
> - **Heart:** 
>   - **Desire:** "To prove that the old ways of magic are weak. To create a new, stronger form of life from corruption."
>   - **Fear:** "Being seen as a failure. Having his work destroyed before it can 'bloom.'"
> - **Talents:** *Twisted Rite*, *Siphon Spirit*.
> - **Rites Known:** *Bramble Ward* (entangle area, opposed check, 2 Aura), *Whisper the Sprout* (twisted version: accelerate blight, TN 7, 2 Aura), *Rootbind* (custom: target must succeed on TN 9 Grace check or be Knocked Down and take 1 Wound, 3 Aura).

### The Confrontation

Sylas turns as the party enters. He is gaunt, his skin marked with blackened veins. He does not attack immediately.

**Parley Opportunity:**
Sylas is arrogant and believes his work is righteous. The party can attempt to Parley:
- **Appeal to his Desire:** "Your work is fascinating, but it's killing innocent people. Can't you find a way to perfect it without destruction?" (TN 9, as he is fanatical).
- **Exploit his Fear:** "The village has sent for the Wardens. They'll destroy your work and you'll be remembered as a failure." (TN 7, as he fears this deeply).

**Parley Outcomes:**
- **High Crit (Boon):** Sylas is intrigued. He agrees to halt the blight and work with the Herbalist to find a cure, if the party helps him gather rare components (a new Adventure hook).
- **Success:** Sylas agrees to leave, but only if the party allows him to take a sample of the blighted root with him. (Moral dilemma: do they let the corruption spread elsewhere?)
- **Failure:** Sylas sneers. "You are small-minded. You will die with the rest of them." Combat begins. Sylas gains **Posture**: he immediately casts *Bramble Ward* (entangling the area, TN 7 to break free).
- **Fumble (Bane):** Sylas laughs. "Fools." He casts *Rootbind* on the party leader (TN 9 Grace check or take 1 Wound and be Knocked Down). Combat begins with the party at a disadvantage.

### Combat: The Blighted Grove

If combat begins, the grove is a **tactical nightmare**:
- **Environmental Hazard:** The blackened vines are **Difficult Terrain** (−1 step to Movement and attacks). A player can spend an Action to clear a 1-Zone path (TN 7 Might or Grace check).
- **Sylas's Tactics:** He stays in the **Far** zone, using *Bramble Ward* to entangle the party and *Rootbind* to Knock Down targets. He uses *Twisted Rite* to auto-crit his spells, but each use causes a horrific side-effect (GM rolls on the Dissonance table, but the results are more severe: the ground erupts with thorns, the air fills with choking spores, etc.).
- **Minions:** If Sylas is reduced to half Vitality, he animates 2 **Blighted Wolves** (Track 2 Standard) from the corpses of previous victims.

**Environmental Interaction Opportunities:**
- The standing stones are ancient and resonant. A player can spend an Action to channel their Aura into a stone (TN 9 Resolve check) to dispel the blight in a 1-Zone area, removing the Difficult Terrain and granting all allies +1 step on attacks against Sylas.
- The pulsing root at the center is the source. A player can attack it (TN 9 Grace check, −1 step for Called Shot) to weaken Sylas. If the root is destroyed, Sylas loses 1d4 Aura and takes 1 Wound.

### The Climax

When Sylas is reduced to 0 Vitality, he does not die immediately. He uses his **Last Stand** (Track 2 Elite rule, but applied here for drama): he takes 1 Wound and makes one final, desperate cast of *Whisper the Sprout*, causing the blighted root to erupt and attempt to drag him into the earth.

The party must decide:
- **Let him go:** Sylas disappears into the earth, vowing to return. (He becomes a recurring villain.)
- **Destroy the root:** The blight is halted, but Sylas is buried alive. (Moral consequence: the villagers are grateful, but the party wonders if they could have saved him.)
- **Capture him:** If a player succeeds on a TN 9 Might or Grace check, they can restrain Sylas and bring him to justice. (He becomes a prisoner, and the party must decide what to do with him.)

## Part 4: Resolution

### If the Blight is Halted

The village rejoices. Elder Maren offers the party the 30 Crowns, plus:
- **Trade Opportunity:** The village's remaining Provisions are now safe to sell. The party can make a Commerce Check (TN 7) to buy low and sell high in the next Metropolis they visit.
- **Reputation:** The party gains +1 step on all social checks in this region for the next 3 Adventures.
- **Heart Trigger:** If a character's **Desire** was to help the village, they fulfill it and can archive it, gaining +1 Max Aura, +2 Max Vitality, or 1 Milestone.

### If the Blight Continues

If Sylas escapes or the root is not destroyed, the blight spreads. The village is abandoned within a week. The party:
- Gains no reward.
- Suffers a **Bane**: the villagers curse them, and the party gains −1 step on all social checks in this region.
- Sylas becomes a recurring villain, and the blight spreads to neighboring villages, creating a larger crisis for future Adventures.

### Epilogue

As the party leaves Ashenford, ask each player:
- **What did you learn about yourself?**
- **What will you do with the knowledge of the Corruptor?**
- **How has your character changed?**

This is a perfect moment to trigger the **Heart system**: if a character gained a new Fear during the adventure (e.g., "Fear of the Blight"), they can choose to confront it in a future Adventure. If they succeed, they archive it and grow stronger.

## GM Notes & Tips

### Pacing
- **Day 1:** Smooth travel, establish the tone, introduce the Trade system.
- **Day 2:** Complication (Blighted Wolf), demonstrate tactical combat and environmental interaction.
- **Day 3:** Investigation, social encounters, build tension.
- **Part 3:** The climax. Parley or combat, both should feel weighty and consequential.
- **Part 4:** Resolution, tie up loose ends, set up future Adventures.

### Scaling
- If the party is **Adventure Level 2 or higher**, increase Sylas's Vitality to 30 and give him one additional Rite.
- If the party is small (1–2 players), reduce the Blighted Wolves to **Nuisance** tier (Vitality 12).
- If the party is large (5–6 players), add a second Corruptor or give Sylas 2 additional Aura.

### Hooks for Future Adventures
- **Sylas Escapes:** He returns with a larger operation, corrupting an entire forest.
- **The Root Sample:** If the party let Sylas take a sample, the blight appears in a distant city, and they must track him down.
- **The Wardens:** If the party mentioned the Wardens, they arrive and question the party's methods. Are the Wardens allies or antagonists?
- **The Village's Gratitude:** Ashenford becomes a safe haven for the party, offering discounts, rumors, and a place to rest between Adventures.

## Appendix: Quick Reference for This Adventure

### Key NPCs
- **Elder Maren:** Desire = "Save her people." Fear = "Being remembered as a failure."
- **Sylas the Root-Twisted:** Desire = "Prove the old ways are weak." Fear = "Being seen as a failure."

### Key Locations
- **Ashenford:** Agrarian tag. Provisions cheap (but blighted), Crafted expensive.
- **Blighted Grove:** Difficult Terrain, environmental hazards, standing stones can be used to dispel blight.

### Key Checks
- **Investigation:** TN 7 Wit + Lore or Resolve + Empathy.
- **Parley with Sylas:** TN 7 (exploit Fear) or TN 9 (appeal to Desire).
- **Environmental Interaction (Standing Stones):** TN 9 Resolve check.
- **Attack the Blighted Root:** TN 9 Grace check (Called Shot).
