# RULES v 0.1

## 1. The Premise

The world of **Dantor** is vast, old, and mostly peaceful. Empires have risen and crumbled into hills covered with wildflowers. What remains are the roads. The player characters are **Wayfarers** — folk who walk those roads for a living: couriers, pilgrims, cartographers, mages, retired soldiers, curious children with too much courage.

Games are played in **Journeys**: a Journey is one expedition from town to town, lasting a handful of sessions. Between Journeys, characters rest, trade, learn, and grow. No single story needs to end in a dragon's lair; many end with a bridge repaired and a village's granary full before winter.

### Play Pillars
1. **The road is the adventure.** Weather, terrain, hunger, and getting lost are as important as any foe.
2. **Combat is short and avoidable.** Running, bargaining, and cleverness beat fighting.
3. **Failure moves the story forward.** A bad roll should open a new door, not close one.
4. **Everyone keeps a journal.** Notes, sketches, and small mementos are part of play.

## 2. Characters

A Wayfarer is built from three parts:

| Part | Question it answers | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Facet die** (x4) | What are you naturally good at? | See §3 |
| **Discipline** | How do you approach problems? | Vanguard, Savant, Wanderer |
| **Calling** | What do you actually *do* on the road? | Courier, Herbalist, Lamplighter, Cartographer, Beast-keeper, Miner, Chef, Bell-warden… |

### Facets
Four Facets, each expressed as a die:
- **Might** — strength, endurance, physical daring
- **Grace** — agility, balance, steady hands
- **Wit** — knowledge, perception, cunning
- **Resolve** — willpower, faith, composure, magic affinity

Facet dice range from **d4 (weak) to d12 (legendary)** in even steps: d4, d6, d8, d10, d12. Starting characters distribute facets per their Discipline (§4).

### Wear & Tear (Health)
Wayfarers track **Tear**, a count of wounds and exhaustion:
- **0–5 Tear:** Steady. No penalty.
- **6–9 Tear:** Battered. All checks −1 step on one Facet (chosen when first injured; shifts if further injured).
- **10+ Tear:** Gone. The Wayfarer collapses and must be carried to safety; if untreated by the next sunrise, they die.

Tear heals naturally: **1 point per day of ordinary rest, 2 points per night at a proper camp** (§8), more with herbs or care. Magic and medicine can accelerate this.

### Aura (Push Pool)
A single resource combining effort and spirit. Starting Aura = **Discipline base + Resolve die value**.
Spend 1 Aura to:
- Re-roll one check (keep the second result).
- Add +2 to any single roll after seeing it.
- Fuel a Calling trick or Rite that requires "effort."
- Push through Injury: ignore your Battered penalty for one scene.

Aura recovers fully after a night of good sleep and food. Stressful journeys may only restore half.

### Resonance (Magic Affinity)
Characters who use magic attune to one of the **Five Truths** at creation or level-up: **Ember (fire/change), Tide (water/perception), Root (plant/growth), Gale (wind/creativity), Stone (earth/endurance)**. Resonance grants passive traits and access to Rites (§7). Non-magical characters may still learn simple Charms via certain Callings.

## 3. Core Resolution — The Pair Roll

When the outcome is uncertain and failure has stakes:

> **Roll your Facet die + your Craft die, compare to the Target Number (TN).**

- **Craft die:** every character has a Craft die starting at **d6**. If an action clearly falls under your Discipline or a relevant Calling, your Craft die steps up (see Skill Ladder below).
- **Target Numbers:** Routine **TN 5** · Demanding **TN 7** · Formidable **TN 9** · Heroic **TN 11**. Default is TN 7.
- **Skill Ladder:** instead of flat bonuses, relevant training upgrades dice *steps*: untrained d6 → trained d8 → expert d10 → master d12. Circumstances add or remove whole steps (good tools +1 step, darkness −1 step).

### Criticals & Fumbles
- **High Crit:** both dice show their maximum → success plus a **Boon** (GM narrates a bonus: extra effect, time saved, a lead).
- **Low Crit:** both dice show 6 (even on non-d6s) → success with style; treat as crit if either crit rule fits.
- **Fumble:** both dice show 1 → failure plus an **Ill** (complication, cost, or new danger — never a dead end).

### Opposed Checks
Both parties roll; higher total wins, ties favor the defender/incumbent state.

### Group Checks
One Wayfarer leads; each participant with a relevant skill adds +1 step to the leader's roll (max +2 steps). Others may simply roll their own and take the better shared result if the fiction allows cooperation.

## 4. Disciplines

Each Discipline sets starting Facets, Aura base, and a signature combat stance.

| Discipline | Starting Facets | Aura base | Signature trait |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Vanguard** | Might d8, Grace d6, Wit d4, Resolve d6 | 3 | **Guard:** once per scene, make one attack against an ally within reach harder to land (attacker rolls −1 step). |
| **Savant** | Might d4, Grace d6, Wit d8, Resolve d8 | 4 | **Insight:** spend 1 Aura to learn one true fact about a person, place, or thing. |
| **Wanderer** | Might d6, Grace d8, Wit d6, Resolve d6 | 3 | **Pathwise:** once per session, declare a helpful detail about the locale that becomes true (a ford here, a market day tomorrow). |

Choose a **Calling** from the list (§6) — it defines your trained Craft die and Skills.

---

## 5. Advancement

Characters gain **Marks** at the end of each Journey (typically 1–3):
- Spend 1 Mark: +1 step to a Calling-linked Craft ladder (max d12).
- Spend 2 Marks: +1 step to a Facet (max d12).
- Spend 3 Marks: gain a new Calling trick, a new Rite, or a second Attunement.
- At every 3rd Journey completed, all characters gain **+1 max Aura** and **+1 Tear threshold headroom** is NOT granted (Tear stays lethal by design).

Level equivalents: Journey count doubles as experience. GMs who prefer XP may award 1,000 XP per completed Journey plus 100 per major milestone; 1 Mark = 1,000 XP.

## 6. Callings (Examples)

Each Calling grants: **Trained Craft (d8)**, two Skills, and starting gear.

- **Courier** — Trained: running, navigation, haggling. Skills: *Express Route* (once per journey, move double pace without a March check); *Read the Crowd*.
- **Herbalist** — Trained: healing, lore (plants), craft. Skills: *Salve* (heal 2 Tear once per day); *Poultice Purge* (neutralize one venom/sickness).
- **Lamplighter** — Trained: stealth, tinker, notice. Skills: *Darkward Lantern* (light cone that only allies see); *Night Eyes*.
- **Cartographer** — Trained: wayfinding, drawing, history. Skills: *True Map* (cannot be Lost this Journey); *Survey Note* (reveal hidden feature once per site).
- **Beast-keeper** — Trained: animals, handling, tracking. Skills: *Pack Bond* (mount/animal gains +1 step on its checks); *Hush the Hounds*.
- **Miner** (Resonant) — Trained: rites (Stone), listening-to-stone, endurance. Skills: *Quicken Clay*; *Earthfast* (ally ignores next Knockdown).
- **Chef** — Trained: cooking, comfort, herb-lore. Skills: *Warm Meal* (party recovers full Aura after camp); *Stretch the Pot* (feeds any number of mouths for one meal).
- **Bell-warden** (Resonant) — Trained: rites (Gale/Tide), vigilance, song. Skills: *Alarm Toll*; *Carry the Tune* (spell persists silently until needed).

New Callings should follow the template: one wordy pitch, Trained list, exactly two Skills, kit.

## 7. Magic — Rites & Pillars

Magic is slow, costly, and wondrous. Casters are called **Attuned**.

### Casting
To cast a **Rite**: spend Aura equal to the Rite's Cost, then make a **Resolve + Craft (Rites)** check vs. the Rite's TN. On a fumble, roll on the **Dissonance table** (§7.3).

### Rite Lists by Pillar (sample tier 1)
- **Ember:** *Kindler's Kiss* (ignite something flammable, TN 5, 1 Aura); *Forge-Sense* (know temperature/composition of metal, TN 6, 1).
- **Tide:** *Mend Small Wrongs* (heal 1d6 Tear, TN 7, 2); *Still Water* (purify a pool, TN 5, 1).
- **Root:** *Whisper the Sprout* (grow a plant a season in minutes, TN 6, 1); *Bramble Ward* (entangle area, opposed check, 2).
- **Gale:** *Lantern Wind* (carry sound or scent across ~30m, TN 5, 1); *Featherstep* (ignore one difficult-terrain penalty this scene, TN 6, 1).
- **Stone:** *Steady Ground* (target cannot be knocked prone, TN 6, 1); *Door Whisper* (learn what passed through a doorway in last day, TN 7, 2).

Higher tiers (Cost 3–5) exist per pillar; GMs approve custom Rites using the same template: Name / Cost / TN / Duration / Tell (visible side-effect).

### Dissonance Table (d6)
1. The Rite works but your next cast this scene costs +1 Aura.
2. Local weather mirrors your mood for 1 hour.
3. Your Tell becomes permanent-ish for a day (hair smokes, voice echoes…).
4. You lose 1 additional Aura.
5. Something notices you — a spirit, a watcher, the land itself.
6. Rite fails and the material component is consumed.

## 8. The Journey System

Travel is a structured turn cycle, one round per day of walking.

### Daily Cycle
1. **March Check** — led by whoever handles pace (often Wanderer/Courier). TN set by Terrain Level (§8.2). Success: party covers the day's planned distance. Failure: shorter distance or forced detour.
2. **Wayfinding Check** — TN based on region ambiguity. Failure: **Lost** (see below).
3. **Encampment Check** — finding safe shelter. Success: camp established (full recovery, +2 Tear/day healing). Failure: exposed camp (half recovery) or worse weather overnight.
4. **Watch & Wonder** — GM resolves encounters, weather shifts, and downtime activities.

### Getting Lost
On a failed Wayfinding check, the party is Lost: subtract one Terrain Level worth of progress per day until they regain bearings (new Wayfinding check at +1 TN, or a landmark, map, or local guide helps).

### Terrain Levels
| Level | Name | March TN | Pace (km/day) | Example |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Green Road | 5 | 24 | Maintained lowland highway |
| 2 | Open Country | 6 | 20 | Fields, gentle hills |
| 3 | Wild Downs | 7 | 16 | Moor, forest edge |
| 4 | Hard Lands | 8 | 12 | Hills, thick woods, marsh |
| 5 | Grievous Ground | 9 | 8 | Mountains, floodplain, deep snow |

### Weather & Seasons
Weather is rolled or chosen daily: Clear (+0), Overcast (+0), Rain/Wind (all checks −1 step), Storm (−1 step and Encampment TN +2), Fog (Wayfinding −1 step). Seasons shift pace ±2 km/day and flip which extremes occur (winter storms, summer droughts).

### Party Modifiers
- 3–5 Wayfarers: +0. Fewer than 3: −1 step on Encampment. More than 6: −1 step on March (herding people is slow).
- **Load:** light +0, normal +0, heavy −1 step on March, overloaded −2 and risk dropping items.
- **Mounts/animals:** +1 step on March if enough pack/beast support for the party size.

## 9. Encounters & Combat

Combat uses the same Pair Roll. It is designed to last **2–4 rounds**.

### Zones & Hit Places
Simple abstract hit locations (optional tactical layer): Head, Heart, Arms, Legs, Body. Called shots: −1 step but bypass armor slot or cause a specific consequence (disarm, knockdown, bleed). Armor covers one zone; Shields cover Arms/Body choice.

### Attack & Damage
- **Attack:** Facet (Might or Grace) + Craft vs. target's **Evasion** (10 + best relevant defense; armed defenders add shield/weapon parry as +1 step).
- **Damage:** weapon die + Facet die − armor value. Weapons: dagger d4 … longsword d8 … poleaxe d10 … bow d6 (ranged). Armor: padded 2, mail 4, plate 6 (heavy load).
- **Injury:** damage over target's remaining Tear threshold causes Knockdown (skip next turn) then Gone.

### Negotiation First
Every hostile group has a **Price** (what they want) and a **Fear** (what they avoid). Meeting Price or Fear ends the fight without dice. GMs should telegraph these.

### Fleeing
Group March-style check vs. pursuer's Grace check; success escapes, failure means caught with enemies gaining +1 step next round.

## 10. The Game Master

The GM is a **neutral referee**, not a player and not a deity. Duties:
1. Describe Dantor honestly; rules adjudication is impartial.
2. Track weather, terrain, supplies, and rumor clocks.
3. Award Marks and Hope (below) according to published triggers, not favoritism.
4. Never roll against players secretly when open dice would serve drama better.

### Hope Dice (table currency)
Any player may hold **Hope tokens** (start each session with 1; earn more when their failure makes a better story, when they embody their Calling's spirit, or when they help another PC shine). Spend 1 Hope to:
- Step up one die in a Pair Roll after rolling.
- Refuse a Ill once per scene.
- Give another player's check +1 step before rolling.

The GM awards Hope openly and neutrally; there is no meta-player figure, no divine dice, no cosmic scorekeeper.

## 11. Gear & Economy

Currency: **shillings** (common), **marks** (1 mark = 10 shillings).
Sample prices: bread 1 sp · rope 3 sp · lantern 8 sp · mail 60 sp · warhorse 250 sp · herbal kit 12 sp.

Containers carry capacity in **burden**; exceeding personal burden (Might value × 2 kg baseline) imposes Load penalties (§8). Mules, carts, and trained dogs expand capacity.

Special materials: **Kirksteel** (−1 step to craft, +1 armor/damage cap), **Sunwood** (light, springy bows), **River-glass** (holds enchantments cheaply). These replace exotic-metal tropes.

---

## 12. Safety Tools & Table Practices

Recommended: lines & veils, a session-zero discussion of tone (moderate difficulty — hardship yes, cruelty no), and the Ill principle ("fail forward") baked into the rules.

## Appendix A — Quick Reference Card

- Pair Roll: **Facet + Craft ≥ TN** (5/7/9/11).
- Crit: max-max or 6-6 → Boon. Fumble: 1-1 → Ill.
- Tear: 0–5 fine · 6–9 battered (−1 step) · 10 gone.
- Aura: re-roll, +2, fuel Skills/Rites, shrug off injury.
- Daily: March → Wayfind → Camp → Watch & Wonder.
- Hope: spend to step up dice, refuse complications, aid allies.
