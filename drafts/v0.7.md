# RULES v 0.7

## 1. The Premise

The world of **Dantor** is vast, old, and mostly peaceful. Empires have risen and crumbled into hills covered with wildflowers. What remains are the roads. 

The player characters are **Wayfarers** — folk who walk those roads, wherever they may lead. Whether you are a pilgrim trekking across the continent, an immigrant navigating the winding alleys of a capital city, or a settler building a new life in a quiet countryside village, you are a Wayfarer. 

Games are played in **Adventures**: an Adventure is a distinct chapter of play, lasting a handful of sessions. Between Adventures, characters rest, trade, learn, and grow. No single story needs to end in a dragon's lair; many end with a bridge repaired, a political rival outmaneuvered, or a village's granary full before winter. Life itself is the adventure.

### Play Pillars
1. **The path is the adventure.** Whether navigating wild terrain, city politics, or village life, the environment and daily challenges are as important as any foe.
2. **Conflict is weighty, but avoidable.** Fights are tactical and continue until resolved, but running, bargaining, and cleverness often beat fighting.
3. **Failure moves the story forward.** A bad roll should open a new door, not close one.
4. **Everyone keeps a journal.** Notes, sketches, and small mementos are part of play.

## 2. Characters

A Wayfarer is built from four parts:

| Part | Question it answers | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Facet die** (x4) | What are you naturally good at? | See §3 |
| **Discipline** | How do you approach problems? | Vanguard, Savant, Wanderer |
| **Job** | What is your role in the world? | Courier, Herbalist, Lamplighter, Cartographer… |
| **Heart** | What drives and haunts you? | Desire, Fear |

### Facets
Four Facets, each expressed as a die:
- **Might** — strength, endurance, physical daring
- **Grace** — agility, balance, steady hands
- **Wit** — knowledge, perception, cunning
- **Resolve** — willpower, faith, composure, magic affinity

Facet dice range from **d4 (weak) to d12 (legendary)** in even steps: d4, d6, d8, d10, d12. Starting characters distribute facets per their Discipline (§4).

### Health: Vitality & Wounds
Wayfarers track their physical and mental endurance using two distinct tracks: **Vitality** (their HP buffer) and **Wounds** (actual injuries and exhaustion).

**Vitality (HP):** Represents stamina, luck, adrenaline, and minor scrapes. 
*   **Max Vitality:** `(Might Die Value + Resolve Die Value) + Discipline Base + (Adventure Level × 2)`.
*   *Example:* A Vanguard (Base 3) with Might d8 and Resolve d6 at Adventure Level 1 has `8 + 6 + 3 + 2 = 19 Max Vitality`.
*   **Recovery:** Vitality recovers quickly. A short rest recovers half, and a full night's sleep recovers all Vitality.

**Wounds:** Represents deep injuries, trauma, and exhaustion. When Vitality reaches 0, excess damage becomes Wounds. Certain devastating enemy attacks may also inflict Wounds directly.
*   **0–5 Wounds:** Steady. No penalty.
*   **6–9 Wounds:** Battered. All checks −1 step on one Facet (chosen when first injured; shifts if further injured).
*   **10+ Wounds:** Down. The Wayfarer collapses and must be carried to safety; if untreated by the next sunrise, they die.
*   **Recovery:** Wounds heal slowly. **1 point per day of ordinary rest, 2 points per night at a proper camp or safe lodging** (§8). Magic and the Herbalist's care can accelerate this.

### Aura & The Spark (Push Pool)
Aura represents a Wayfarer's spirit, effort, and momentum. 
*   **Max Aura:** `Discipline base + Resolve die value`.
*   **Recovery:** Aura recovers fully after a night of good sleep and food. Stressful times may only restore half.

**Spending Aura:** Spend 1 Aura to:
- Re-roll one check (keep the second result).
- Add +2 to any single roll after seeing it.
- Fuel a Job Talent or Rite that requires "effort."
- Push through Injury: ignore your Battered penalty for one scene.
- **React:** Use a defensive Reaction in combat (see §9).

**Generating Aura (The Spark):** Characters can generate **1 Aura Point (AP)** dynamically during play when the following triggers occur:
1. **Turn Failure into Fuel:** You roll a Fumble (1-1) or fail a check by a narrow margin.
2. **Exploit the Narrative:** You successfully leverage an enemy’s "Fear" or "Desire" during a Parley or conflict.
3. **Camaraderie:** Another player spends a Hope token to assist your roll, or you spend one to assist theirs.
4. **Momentum:** You reduce an enemy to 0 Vitality, or the party successfully completes a major Adventure milestone.

### Heart: Desire & Fear (The Evolving Heart)
Every Wayfarer is driven by something and running from something. During character creation, define your **Core Desire** (what you crave, your ultimate motivation) and your **Core Fear** (what paralyzes you, your trauma or phobia). 

You may have a maximum of **two active Desires** and **two active Fears** at any time.

**Gaining Scars & Ambitions:** 
When you take a **Wound** or suffer a severe **Bane**, the GM may offer you a narrative bargain: accept a new Fear related to that traumatic moment (e.g., "Fear of the deep caves," "Fear of this specific rival," "Fear of the city guard"). If you accept, you immediately gain **1 Aura** or **1 Hope Token**. Similarly, achieving great triumphs or rolling a Boon on a passion project can cement a new Desire.

**Overcoming:** Fears and Desires are meant to be confronted, not hoarded. If you actively face your Fear and succeed in a dramatic moment, or fulfill your Desire, you **archive** it. In doing so, you gain a permanent boon: **+1 Max Aura**, **+2 Max Vitality**, or **1 Milestone**. You then write a new Desire or Fear, reflecting your character's growth.

### Resonance (Magic Affinity)
Characters who use magic attune to one of the **Five Truths** at creation or level-up: **Ember (fire/change), Tide (water/perception), Root (plant/growth), Gale (wind/creativity), Stone (earth/endurance)**. Resonance grants passive traits and access to Rites (§7). Non-magical characters may still learn simple Charms via certain Jobs.

## 3. Core Resolution — The Paired Roll

When the outcome is uncertain and failure has stakes:

> **Roll your Facet die + your Craft die, compare to the Target Number (TN).**

- **Craft die:** every character has a Craft die starting at **d6**. If an action clearly falls under your Discipline or a relevant Job, your Craft die steps up (see Craft Ladder below).
- **Target Numbers:** Routine **TN 5** · Demanding **TN 7** · Formidable **TN 9** · Heroic **TN 11**. Default is TN 7.
- **Craft Ladder:** instead of flat bonuses, relevant training upgrades dice *steps*: untrained d6 → trained d8 → expert d10 → master d12. Circumstances add or remove whole steps (good tools +1 step, darkness −1 step).

### Criticals & Fumbles
- **High Crit:** both dice show their maximum → success plus a **Boon** (GM narrates a bonus: extra effect, time saved, a lead).
- **Low Crit:** both dice show 6 (even on non-d6s) → success with style; treat as crit if either crit rule fits.
- **Fumble:** both dice show 1 → failure plus a **Bane** (complication, cost, or new danger — never a dead end).

### Opposed Checks
Both parties roll; higher total wins, ties favor the defender/incumbent state.

### Group Checks
One Wayfarer leads; each participant with a relevant Craft adds +1 step to the leader's roll (max +2 steps). Others may simply roll their own and take the better shared result if the fiction allows cooperation.

## 4. Disciplines

Each Discipline sets starting Facets, Aura base, and a signature stance.

| Discipline | Starting Facets | Aura base | Signature trait |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Vanguard** | Might d8, Grace d6, Wit d4, Resolve d6 | 3 | **Guard:** once per scene, make one attack against an adjacent ally harder to land (attacker rolls −1 step) without spending Aura. |
| **Savant** | Might d4, Grace d6, Wit d8, Resolve d8 | 4 | **Insight:** spend 1 Aura to learn one true fact about a person, place, or thing. |
| **Wanderer** | Might d6, Grace d8, Wit d6, Resolve d6 | 3 | **Pathwise:** once per session, declare a helpful detail about the locale that becomes true (a hidden ford, a sympathetic guard, a market day). |

Choose a **Job** from the list (§6) — it defines your trained Craft die and Talents.

---

## 5. Advancement

Characters gain **Milestones** at the end of each Adventure (typically 1–3):
- Spend 1 Milestone: +1 step to a Job-linked Craft ladder (max d12).
- Spend 2 Milestones: +1 step to a Facet (max d12).
- Spend 3 Milestones: gain a new Job Talent, a new Rite, or a second Attunement.

**Adventure Levels:** At the end of every 3rd completed Adventure, all characters advance to the next **Adventure Level**, gaining **+1 Max Aura** and **+2 Max Vitality**. 

*(Note: GMs who prefer traditional XP may award 1,000 XP per completed Adventure plus 100 per major milestone; 1 Milestone = 1,000 XP).*

## 6. Jobs (Examples)

Each Job grants: **Trained Craft (d8)**, two Talents, and starting gear.

- **Courier** — Trained: running, navigation, haggling. Talents: *Express Route* (once per adventure, move double pace or bypass a bureaucratic delay without a check); *Read the Crowd*.
- **Herbalist** — Trained: healing, lore (plants), craft. Talents: *Salve* (heal 2 Wounds once per day); *Poultice Purge* (neutralize one venom/sickness).
- **Lamplighter** — Trained: stealth, tinker, notice. Talents: *Darkward Lantern* (light cone that only allies see); *Night Eyes*.
- **Cartographer** — Trained: wayfinding, drawing, history. Talents: *True Map* (cannot be Lost this adventure); *Survey Note* (reveal hidden feature once per site).
- **Beast-keeper** — Trained: animals, handling, tracking. Talents: *Pack Bond* (mount/animal gains +1 step on its checks); *Hush the Hounds*.
- **Miner** (Resonant) — Trained: rites (Stone), listening-to-stone, endurance. Talents: *Quicken Clay*; *Earthfast* (ally ignores next Knockdown).
- **Chef** — Trained: cooking, comfort, herb-lore. Talents: *Warm Meal* (party recovers full Aura after resting); *Stretch the Pot* (feeds any number of mouths for one meal).
- **Bell-warden** (Resonant) — Trained: rites (Gale/Tide), vigilance, song. Talents: *Alarm Toll*; *Carry the Tune* (spell persists silently until needed).

New Jobs should follow the template: one wordy pitch, Trained list, exactly two Talents, kit.

## 7. Magic — Rites & Pillars

Magic is slow, costly, and wondrous. Casters are called **Attuned**.

### Casting
To cast a **Rite**: spend Aura equal to the Rite's Cost, then make a **Resolve + Craft (Rites)** check vs. the Rite's TN. On a fumble, roll on the **Dissonance table** (§7.3).

### Rite Lists by Pillar (sample tier 1)
- **Ember:** *Kindler's Kiss* (ignite something flammable, TN 5, 1 Aura); *Forge-Sense* (know temperature/composition of metal, TN 6, 1).
- **Tide:** *Mend Small Wrongs* (heal 1d6 Vitality, TN 7, 2); *Still Water* (purify a pool, TN 5, 1).
- **Root:** *Whisper the Sprout* (grow a plant a season in minutes, TN 6, 1); *Bramble Ward* (entangle area, opposed check, 2).
- **Gale:** *Lantern Wind* (carry sound or scent across ~30m, TN 5, 1); *Featherstep* (ignore one difficult-terrain penalty this scene, TN 6, 1).
- **Stone:** *Steady Ground* (target cannot be knocked prone, TN 6, 1); *Door Whisper* (learn what passed through a doorway in last day, TN 7, 2).

Higher tiers (Cost 3–5) exist per pillar; GMs approve custom Rites using the same template: Name / Cost / TN / Duration / Tell (visible side-effect).

### Dissonance Table (d6)
1. The Rite works but your next cast this scene costs +1 Aura.
2. Local weather (or mood of the room) mirrors your mood for 1 hour.
3. Your Tell becomes permanent-ish for a day (hair smokes, voice echoes…).
4. You lose 1 additional Aura.
5. Something notices you — a spirit, a watcher, the land itself.
6. Rite fails and the material component is consumed.

## 8. The Adventure System

Adventures are structured in cycles. When traveling, this is one round per day of walking. When settled in a city or village, the GM adapts the cycle to fit the pacing of the location.

### The Daily Cycle (Travel)
1. **March Check** — led by whoever handles pace. TN set by Terrain Level (§8.2). Success: party covers the day's planned distance. Failure: shorter distance or forced detour.
2. **Wayfinding Check** — TN based on region ambiguity. Failure: **Lost** (see below).
3. **Encampment Check** — finding safe shelter. Success: camp established (full recovery, +2 Wounds/day healing). Failure: exposed camp (half recovery) or worse weather overnight.
4. **Watch & Wonder** — GM resolves encounters, weather shifts, and downtime activities.

### Adapting the Cycle (Static Locations)
If the party is based in a city or village, the GM replaces the travel checks with location-appropriate checks:
- **March** becomes *Networking/Scouting* (gathering rumors, finding contacts).
- **Wayfinding** becomes *Gathering Information* (navigating bureaucracy, finding a specific person).
- **Encampment** becomes *Securing Lodging* (finding a safe inn, avoiding city watch patrols).

### Getting Lost
On a failed Wayfinding (or Gathering Information) check, the party is Lost: they waste time and resources until they regain their bearings (new check at +1 TN, or a landmark, map, or local guide helps).

### Terrain Levels (or District Complexity)
| Level | Name | Check TN | Pace / Progress | Example |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Green Road / Safe Ward | 5 | High | Maintained highway / Friendly village |
| 2 | Open Country / Bustling Market | 6 | Moderate | Fields, gentle hills / Crowded city streets |
| 3 | Wild Downs / Undercity | 7 | Slow | Moor, forest edge / Sewers, slums |
| 4 | Hard Lands / Noble Quarter | 8 | Very Slow | Thick woods, marsh / Heavily guarded estates |
| 5 | Grievous Ground / Forbidden Zone | 9 | Minimal | Mountains, deep snow / Restricted magical ruins |

### Weather & Seasons
Weather is rolled or chosen daily: Clear (+0), Overcast (+0), Rain/Wind (all checks −1 step), Storm (−1 step and Encampment TN +2), Fog (Wayfinding −1 step). Seasons shift pace ±2 km/day and flip which extremes occur (winter storms, summer droughts). In cities, "Weather" can represent social climate (e.g., "Festival Season" gives +1 step to social checks, "Martial Law" gives −1 step).

### Party Modifiers
- 3–5 Wayfarers: +0. Fewer than 3: −1 step on Encampment/Lodging. More than 6: −1 step on March/Networking (herding people is slow/draws attention).
- **Load:** light +0, normal +0, heavy −1 step on March, overloaded −2 and risk dropping items.
- **Mounts/animals:** +1 step on March if enough pack/beast support for the party size.

## 9. Encounters & Combat

Conflict is weighty and continues until it is definitively resolved: enemies are defeated, flee, or surrender. **Parley is entirely optional.** If the party chooses not to negotiate, or decides talking is too risky, they may simply declare an attack and combat begins normally with no penalty.

### Action Economy
On your turn in combat, you may take:
- **One Movement:** Change your Battlefield Zone (see below).
- **One Action:** Attack, Cast a Rite, Use a Talent, Parley, or Interact with the Environment.
- **One Reaction:** (See Universal Reactions below). You may only take one Reaction per round.

### Battlefield Zones (Positioning)
Combat uses three abstract ranges to keep positioning simple but meaningful:
- **Engaged:** Melee range. You are locked in conflict with a specific foe.
- **Near:** Short range (e.g., across a small room or clearing). You can move to Engaged with your Movement.
- **Far:** Long range. Requires bows, slings, or magic to target. Moving from Far to Near, or Near to Engaged, costs your Movement. 

### Hit Locations (Called Shots)
As an Action, you may target a specific body part (Head, Heart, Arms, Legs, Body). This suffers a **−1 step penalty**, but on a success, it bypasses armor or causes a specific consequence (e.g., disarm, knockdown, bleed) at the GM's discretion.

### Universal Reactions
When an enemy targets you with an attack, you may spend **1 Aura** as a Reaction to:
- **Parry/Dodge:** Add +1 step to your Evasion against this specific attack.
- **Intercept:** Step in to take the hit for an adjacent ally (you suffer the damage/Wounds instead).
*(Note: The Vanguard’s 'Guard' Talent allows them to apply the Parry/Dodge effect to an adjacent ally for free once per scene, making them uniquely protective).*

### Environmental Interaction
As an Action, a player can interact with the battlefield (e.g., kicking over a brazier, shoving an enemy toward a cliff edge, using the Chef’s boiling pot, or flipping a tavern table). The GM sets a TN (usually 7). Success grants an ally a +1 step on their next attack against that target, or inflicts a minor Bane on the enemy.

### Parley & Posture (Negotiation)
If chosen, this occurs before initiative is rolled. Every NPC or faction has a **Desire** (what they want) and a **Fear** (what they avoid). 

**PC Parley:** A player declares their approach and makes a Paired Roll (Facet + Craft) against a TN set by the foe’s disposition (**TN 7** Reasonable, **TN 9** Hostile, **TN 11** Fanatic).
*   **High Crit (Max-Max or 6-6):** *Flawless Parley.* Conflict is avoided entirely at no cost. The foe may even offer a minor Boon.
*   **Success:** *Costly Peace.* Conflict is avoided, but you must meet the foe's **Desire** (pay a toll, surrender an item, agree to a favor) or exploit their **Fear** (threaten them into fleeing).
*   **Failure:** *Talk Breaks Down.* Conflict is unavoidable. The foe is now forewarned and prepared. The enemy faction gains **Posture**: they get a free maneuver before the first round, or the party suffers a −1 step penalty on their first round of checks.
*   **Fumble (1-1):** *Disastrous Provocation.* Conflict is unavoidable, and the party is caught at a severe disadvantage. The enemy gains a Surprise round, or the entire party begins combat with 1 Wound due to the stress/ambush.

**NPC Parley (Targeting PC Hearts):**
NPCs will also try to negotiate, tempt, or terrify the Wayfarers by targeting their Desires and Fears. 
*   **Tempting a Desire:** The GM offers the player 1 Hope Token or 1 Aura to willingly accept the NPC's offer and change their course of action. 
*   **Exploiting a Fear:** The GM rolls an Opposed Paired Roll (NPC's Intimidation vs. PC's Resolve). If the NPC wins, the PC must either yield to the fear (retreating or dropping an item, earning 1 Hope) OR push through the panic (suffering a Bane: -1 step to their next action or taking 1 Wound from stress).

### Attack & Damage
- **Attack:** Facet (Might or Grace) + Craft vs. target's **Evasion** (10 + best relevant defense; armed defenders add shield/weapon parry as +1 step).
- **Damage:** `Weapon Die + Facet Bonus - Armor`. 
  - *Facet Bonus:* d4=1, d6=2, d8=3, d10=4, d12=5. 
  - *Weapons:* dagger d4 … longsword d8 … poleaxe d10 … bow d6 (ranged). 
  - *Armor:* padded 2, mail 4, plate 6 (heavy load).
- **Injury:** Damage reduces Vitality. When an attack reduces a character's Vitality to 0, they suffer a **Knockdown** (skip next turn) and any *excess* damage becomes **Wounds**. Additionally, a Critical Hit from an enemy inflicts 1 Wound directly, bypassing Vitality. Reaching 10+ Wounds leaves a character **Down**.

### Fleeing
Group March-style check vs. pursuer's Grace check; success escapes, failure means caught with enemies gaining +1 step next round.

## 10. The Game Master

The GM is a **neutral referee**, not a player and not aDEITY. Duties:
1. Describe Dantor honestly; rules adjudication is impartial.
2. Track weather, terrain, supplies, and rumor clocks (or city gossip clocks).
3. Award Milestones, Aura (via Sparks), and Hope (below) according to published triggers, not favoritism.
4. Never roll against players secretly when open dice would serve drama better.
5. **Weave the Heart:** Actively use the players' Desires and Fears to create compelling roleplay moments, moral dilemmas, and opportunities to earn Hope and Aura.

### Hope Dice (table currency)
Any player may hold **Hope tokens** (start each session with 1; earn more when their failure makes a better story, when they embody their Job's spirit, when they give in to a Tempting Desire, or when they help another PC shine). Spend 1 Hope to:
- Step up one die in a Paired Roll after rolling.
- Refuse a Bane once per scene.
- Give another player's check +1 step before rolling.

The GM awards Hope openly and neutrally; there is no meta-player figure, no divine dice, no cosmic scorekeeper.

## 11. Gear & Economy

Currency: **Crowns** (standard) and **Coppers** (small change, 1 Crown = 10 Coppers).
Sample prices: bread 1 copper · rope 3 coppers · lantern 8 coppers · mail 6 crowns · warhorse 25 crowns · herbal kit 1 crown 2 coppers.

Containers carry capacity in **burden**; exceeding personal burden (Might value × 2 kg baseline) imposes Load penalties (§8). Mules, carts, and trained dogs expand capacity.

Special materials: **Kirksteel** (−1 step to craft, +1 armor/damage cap), **Sunwood** (light, springy bows), **River-glass** (holds enchantments cheaply). These replace exotic-metal tropes.

## 12. Safety Tools & Table Practices

Recommended: lines & veils, a session-zero discussion of tone (moderate difficulty — hardship yes, cruelty no), and the Bane principle ("fail forward") baked into the rules. Because this game utilizes "Fears" and "Trauma," ensure all players are comfortable with psychological pressure elements before introducing the Evolving Heart mechanics.

## Appendix A — Quick Reference Card

- **Paired Roll:** Facet + Craft ≥ TN (5/7/9/11).
- **Crit:** max-max or 6-6 → Boon. **Fumble:** 1-1 → Bane.
- **Vitality:** HP buffer. Heals quickly with rest. 
- **Wounds:** Injuries. 0-5 Steady · 6-9 Battered (-1 step) · 10+ Down. Heals slowly.
- **Aura:** Re-roll, +2, fuel Talents/Rites, or React. Generate 1 AP via Sparks (failing narrowly, exploiting narrative, camaraderie, momentum).
- **Heart:** Max 2 Desires, 2 Fears. Accept a new Fear on trauma to gain 1 Aura/Hope. Overcome a Fear/Desire to gain +1 Max Aura, +2 Max Vitality, or 1 Milestone.
- **Combat Turn:** 1 Movement (change Zone) + 1 Action (Attack, Rite, Talent, Parley, Environment) + 1 Reaction (spend 1 Aura to Parry/Dodge or Intercept).
- **Parley:** Optional. Roll Facet + Craft before conflict. Crit = free avoid · Success = avoid at cost (meet Desire/exploit Fear) · Fail = enemy advantage · Fumble = party disadvantage.
- **Damage:** Weapon Die + Facet Bonus (1 to 5) - Armor. Excess damage at 0 Vitality becomes Wounds.
- **Daily Cycle:** March/Network → Wayfind/Gather Info → Camp/Lodge → Watch & Wonder.
- **Hope:** spend to step up dice, refuse Banes, aid allies.
